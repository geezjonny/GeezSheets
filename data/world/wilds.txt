# WILDS — everything living or lying out there
# + animal breeds and moves.  + plant fills with its seasons and regrows.  + mineral runs out slowly.  + monster is hostile and levels up.
# area: terrains it lives in. food: how filling (becomes a market good). gives: the good it becomes. yields: extra goods. tags: words professions gather by.
# eats: what it hunts ("people" means it hunts townsfolk and travellers). roams: how far it will wander looking for food.
# eats: what it hunts. pack: splits when crowded. solo: one big beast. raids: loots towns. rises: deaths. dark: feeds on strangeness. recruits: outlaws.
# spreads: bite (its victims sicken and turn after "turns" days unless "cured by" a service like health).
# broods: days between clutches for a lone beast (young leave to find their own land). hoards: keeps what it steals in its lair.
# + mutation: what a wild thing can turn into
# FOOD WEB: everything with "eats" shares the food in its area each dawn; stronger hunters take a bigger share.
# Fed and safe animals breed. Hungry, hunted or crowded ones move on and settle elsewhere. Starving ones die back.
# gives: none means people can't gather it (it's only food for animals).
