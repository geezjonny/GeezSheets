# STORIES — what life does to people, and the paths it sends them down
#
# Things that happen to people (the engine marks these in their life story):
#   lost spouse, lost child, lost parent, lost friend, town sacked, came home to ruins, home destroyed,
#   survived raid, mauled, won fight, starving, bitten, married, child born, came of age,
#   became hero, turned outlaw, avenged, redeemed, fled, slew a monster
# Deeds (below) add their own: saved by, robbed by, spared by... anything you write in "marks".
#
# + scar: what an event leaves behind.  from: the events that cause it.  chance: how often (0-1).
#   lasts: days (0 = for good).  work / social / pray: how much more or less they do it.
#   courage: added to their fighting power.  hero: pull toward becoming a hero.  outlaw: pull toward the wilds.
#   leave: pull toward leaving town.  dark: pull toward forbidden things.  grudge: they hold whoever caused it responsible.
#   desire: what they now want. {about} is who caused it, {who} is who they lost.  heals: scars this one ends.
#
# + arc: a path a life can take.  starts: events or scars that can set it off.  also: personalities or wants that make it likelier.
#   steps, in order (the engine knows these):
#     outlaw · gather N (band reaches N) · raid N (N good raids) · take ruins (claims a hideout and renames it) · crown (takes the title)
#     study N (N nights at the graves) · raise one (brings back the one they lost) · hide N · raise all (the next mass death rises with them)
#     become lich · train N · hunt grudge (goes after whoever wronged them) · wait N · found town
#   ends: how it can finish: slain, pardoned, betrayed, redeemed, avenged.   bound: true means their followers fall when they do.
#
# + deed: something the heroes (your players) did. Use it from the "What the heroes did" panel.
#   affects: everyone, some, one (the person you picked), leaders.  marks: what happens to them.
#   kills: how many die (their families grieve, and blame the heroes).  others: what everyone else in town goes through.
#
# + world: how fast lives run.  aging: years of age per year of the world.  childhood: days to grow up.
#   births: more or fewer babies (x).  courting: more or fewer marriages (x).
