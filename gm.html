Paint: click-drag paints/erases one cell at a time (right-click erases). Rect/Line: click-drag from one corner/end to the other. Bucket: click to flood-fill the contiguous same-block area. All four use the selected block below; right-click always erases. Shift+Click (3D view): send a ping.
Geometry (dd2vtt)
Drawn on the 2D panel above (not the 3D view). Wall/Door take the texture of the selected block, click one grid corner then an adjacent one. Light: click a cell. Stairs: click a cell to place one (uses the selected block's texture) -- ↻ Facing and ▦ Shape (stepped or a smooth ramp) set how it looks before you place it. A token walking onto a stair from the correct side changes floor automatically. Erase: click a wall/door/light/stair. Doors also toggle open/closed with a plain click in the 3D view while mode is "None".

Tokens
Token Mode locks out all painting/editing -- click a token (2D panel or 3D view) to select it, then click empty ground to move it. Only the GM or the token's owner can move it. Click "None" in the Geometry panel to leave Token Mode.
