What am I seeing?

MediaPipe Face Landmarker estimates a dense set of 3D facial landmarks. This test draws their 2D positions over the camera image. The model can return up to 478 points, depending on its configuration. Points are normalized to the video frame; z is also available internally.

Privacy: the camera stream is processed in this browser by the page. This demo does not upload or record video. The model and JavaScript library are downloaded from Google’s MediaPipe hosting and jsDelivr when the page runs.
